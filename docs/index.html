Learn to draw cute animals, one easy step at a time

Every Drawsie tutorial breaks a drawing into tiny steps. Just draw the red lines, one panel at a time — if you can draw a circle, you can draw everything here.

How it works

No talent needed. Just a pencil, a piece of paper, and a few minutes.

1

Pick a drawing

Choose an animal from the library below. More are added every week.

2

Follow the red lines

Each step adds just a few new lines, shown in red. Copy those, one step at a time.

3

Color it in

The last step shows the finished drawing in color. Make it yours — any colors you like.
