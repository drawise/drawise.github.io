Tips for young artists

  • Draw lightly at first. Light pencil lines are easy to erase — go over them darker when you're happy.
  • Shapes, not details. Every Drawise drawing starts with simple shapes — circles, ovals, triangles.
  • Mistakes are fine. Wobbly lines make your drawing yours.
  • Any colors work. The last step shows our colors — yours can be completely different.
