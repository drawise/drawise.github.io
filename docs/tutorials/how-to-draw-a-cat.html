Tips for young artists

  • Draw lightly at first. Light pencil lines are easy to erase — go over them darker when you're happy.
  • Shapes, not details. The head is just an oval and the ears are triangles. Every Drawsie drawing starts with simple shapes.
  • Mistakes are fine. Wobbly lines make your cat yours. Even our guide cat is a little wobbly.
  • Any colors work. Cream and pink is our version — a blue cat is a great cat too.
